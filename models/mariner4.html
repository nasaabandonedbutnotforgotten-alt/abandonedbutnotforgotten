Mariner 4

NASA/JPL, 1964–1967 — the first spacecraft to fly past Mars and photograph it up close. 3D model based on the NASA diagram. Hover over a part in the list to highlight it.

    On July 14–15, 1965, Mariner 4 flew about 9,800 km above Mars and sent back 22 pictures — the first close-up images of another planet. Contact ended in December 1967; the spacecraft is still circling the Sun. The model is schematic; proportions are approximate.
