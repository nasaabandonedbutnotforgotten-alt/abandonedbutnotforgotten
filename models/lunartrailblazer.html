Lunar Trailblazer

Caltech / NASA JPL / Lockheed Martin, 2025 — a small satellite built to map water on the Moon. 3D model based on NASA images. Hover over a part in the list to highlight it.

    Contact was lost a day after launch because of an attitude-control software error; the mission was ended on July 31, 2025. The model is schematic; proportions are approximate.
