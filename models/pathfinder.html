Mars Pathfinder & Sojourner

NASA/JPL, 1997 — the lander with its three opened petals and the little Sojourner rover. 3D model based on the NASA diagram. Hover over a part in the list to highlight it.

    Pathfinder bounced onto Mars inside airbags. The lander is now called the Carl Sagan Memorial Station; Sojourner drove about 100 m around it. The model is schematic; proportions are approximate.
