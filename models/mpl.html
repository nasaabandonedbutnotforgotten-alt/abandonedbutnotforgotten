Mars Polar Lander

NASA/JPL, 1999 — 3D model based on the NASA diagram. Hover over a part in the list to highlight it.

    Contact was lost during landing on December 3, 1999. Most likely the engines shut off too early and the lander crashed. Its exact resting place is still unknown. The model is schematic; proportions are approximate.
