Ingenuity Helicopter

The first aircraft to make a controlled flight on another planet (Mars, 2021–2024) — 3D model based on NASA/JPL photos and diagrams. Hover over a part in the list to highlight it.

    Two coaxial 1.2 m rotors spin in opposite directions at about 2,400 rpm — the model shows this in slow motion. The helicopter weighs 1.8 kg and is about 49 cm tall. The model is schematic; proportions are approximate.
