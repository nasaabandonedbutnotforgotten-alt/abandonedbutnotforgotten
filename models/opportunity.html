Opportunity Rover

Mars Exploration Rover (MER-B), NASA/JPL, 2004–2018 — 3D model based on the structure diagram. Hover over a part in the list to highlight it.

    In June 2018 a planet-wide dust storm blocked the Sun, and the dust-covered rover never called home again. The model is schematic; proportions are approximate.
