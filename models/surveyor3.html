NASA robotic lander (1967) — 3D model based on Apollo 12 photos and a NASA diagram. Hover over a part in the list to highlight it.

    The retro rocket and the altitude-marking radar separated during the descent, so the probe stands on the Moon without them. In 1969, Apollo 12 astronauts removed the TV camera and scoop and brought them back to Earth. The model is schematic; proportions are approximate.
