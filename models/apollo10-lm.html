Apollo 10 — Lunar Module “Snoopy” (LM-4)

The lunar module that in May 1969 flew down to about 15.6 km above the Moon — the “dress rehearsal” for the Apollo 11 landing. 3D model based on NASA photos and diagrams. Hover over a part in the list to highlight it.

Launch: May 18, 1969, Saturn V
LM crew: Thomas Stafford and Eugene Cernan
Size: about 7 m tall, legs about 9 m across
Descent stage: dropped in lunar orbit
Ascent stage: after docking its engine was fired until the fuel ran out, and “Snoopy” went into orbit around the Sun — it is still there

    A schematic educational model: the shape and proportions are approximate, not from exact drawings.
