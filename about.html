ABOUT THE PROJECT

Why we care about silent machines

We are third-year cadets majoring in Electronic Communications and Radio Engineering. Every day we study radio communication systems, signal processing, antenna technology and telecommunication networks — the core technologies behind satellite links and deep space exploration.

When a spacecraft runs out of power, loses contact with Earth or finishes its mission, its story does not end. On our website we collect the stories of these machines on the Moon and Mars: where exactly they are, what they did, how they were built and why their missions ended.

Why we joined the hackathon & how we worked

For our six-cadet team, this hackathon is an opportunity to step beyond the classroom, test ourselves under tight deadlines, connect with mentors and build real-world engineering skills.

  • Gain new practical skills beyond regular classroom studies
  • Test ourselves on real challenges under pressure and tight deadlines
  • Learn to work as a team and brainstorm ideas together
  • Connect with experienced engineers, mentors and like-minded peers
  • Step out of our comfort zone and grow professionally and personally
  • Become stronger, bolder and more confident in our abilities

We formed a team of six people (Dayana Vasiukhno, Viktoriia Dashkel, Vitalii Aleksandrovych, Maksym Zinchuk, Denys Sobchuk and Oleksandr Korotkov), defined the concept of the project together and divided responsibilities. Regular sync-ups, testing together and supporting each other helped us turn our idea into a working website.
